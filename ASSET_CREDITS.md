# Meme image sources

The three fan-made Blue Collar SpongeBob meme images below were selected by request. They are downloaded copies of Know Your Meme’s smaller `newsfeed` variants, served from this app’s own asset bundle. No Instagram embeds or external image requests are needed at runtime. The images are not original project artwork and are not represented as covered by a project software license. SpongeBob SquarePants is a Nickelodeon/Paramount character; this fan-made satire is unaffiliated.
